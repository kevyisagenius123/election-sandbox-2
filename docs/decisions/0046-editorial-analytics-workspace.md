# Decision 0046: Editorial analytics workspace

Date: 2026-08-23

## Decision

Sandbox 2.0 will present its accepted scenario and replay analytics inside the existing shared-map laboratory rather than creating a separate analytics page.

The Swingometer will consume the v0.25B scenario-delta ledger for:

- the dominant `What changed?` explanation;
- Actual, Scenario, and Delta values;
- signed operation movement;
- county and reporting-unit rankings;
- operation-specific contribution filters;
- explicit mapped, mixed, and off-map treatment.

Election Night will consume the v0.25C descriptive contract through a visible-replay adapter for:

- five-, fifteen-, and thirty-minute logical windows;
- return, ballot, and margin movement;
- separate published-unit and represented-ballot progress;
- mathematical openness;
- chronology stalls under the user's selected threshold;
- current local movers and exact window ledgers.

## Interface law

- The 3D map remains the primary geographic stage.
- Dense analytics live in the responsive bottom dock and compact editorial drawers.
- React formats and arranges accepted contracts. It does not recalculate analytic truth.
- Unsupported states remain absent from Election Night.
- PA VTD, MI precinct, and WI ward semantics remain distinct.
- Off-map and residual votes remain explicit and are never painted onto invented terrain.
- Mathematical openness is not a forecast or race call.
- No future event or hidden candidate endpoint may influence a visible-prefix diagnostic.

## Consequences

- The useful analytical depth of the old Sandbox returns without its duplicate computation paths or speculative claims.
- Swingometer explanations and Election Night diagnostics now share one Atlas-style visual hierarchy.
- The user can see what changed, where it changed, what has just reported, and whether the synthetic chronology has stalled without leaving the map.
- v0.25 is complete. Calibrated estimates and decision logic remain a separate research gate.
