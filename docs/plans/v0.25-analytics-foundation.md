# v0.25 Plan: Evidence-Graded Analytics Foundation

Date: 2026-08-23

## Objective

Restore the useful analytical depth of the old Sandbox without importing unsupported probability, causality, or decision claims.

The user-facing question is:

> What changed, where did it change, and which explicit assumption or return produced it?

## Sequence

### v0.25A: Headless analytic contract and registry

Create a typed analytic envelope and registry for the values already trusted in Sandbox 2.0.

Initial registered families:

- certified baseline totals and margins;
- scenario endpoint totals and margins;
- requested and realized operation deltas;
- county and reporting-unit signed contributions;
- Electoral College consequences;
- current reported replay totals and margins;
- published-return and represented-ballot progress.

Release gates:

- no UI redesign;
- one semantic class per analytic;
- explicit units and denominators;
- exact serialization and deterministic fingerprints;
- missing values cannot become zero;
- current-prefix analytics cannot contain endpoint outcomes;
- PA, MI, and WI reconcile independently and together.

### v0.25B: Scenario delta ledger

Build a headless before-and-after ledger that attributes the scenario endpoint change to operation, county, reporting unit, and residual geography.

The ledger should answer:

- How many ballots were added, transferred, or exchanged?
- How much Harris-minus-Trump margin movement did each operation produce?
- Which geographies produced the largest positive and negative movements?
- How much movement is outside mapped terrain?
- Which state winner or Electoral College allocation changed because of it?

The existing contribution panel becomes a consumer of this ledger rather than owning ad hoc aggregation.

### v0.25C: Replay descriptive analytics

Add current-only Election Night diagnostics derived from canonical atomic returns:

- newest-return movement;
- movement over selected five-, fifteen-, and thirty-minute logical windows;
- ballots and returns published per window;
- return-unit and modeled-ballot progress as separate measures;
- largest current local margins and largest recent movers;
- mathematically open versus exhausted states;
- explicit chronology stalls under the user-selected synthetic profile.

No final winner, final share, probability, or hidden future return may enter these outputs.

### v0.25D: Editorial analytics workspace

Present the approved ledgers using the Atlas editorial language:

- a dominant `What changed?` summary;
- a signed margin waterfall;
- ranked county and reporting-unit contributors;
- operation filters;
- Actual, Scenario, and Delta columns;
- a compact methodology drawer;
- a responsive bottom-dock form for Election Night.

This restores useful depth without crowding the primary map.

### Later research gate: calibrated estimates

Probability, uncertainty, demographic behavioral estimates, expected-vote projections, and Decision Desk calls remain a separate future research program. They are not part of v0.25.

## Explicit non-goals

v0.25 will not add:

- win probability;
- automated race calls;
- partisan prediction of unreported ballots;
- bellwether or county-importance scores;
- causal demographic claims;
- invented historical reporting chronology;
- a backend requirement;
- a new page disconnected from the Swingometer and shared map.

## Recommended immediate slice

Proceed with v0.25A only. It is the smallest change that prevents future analytics from repeating the old architecture. Do not combine the contract, scenario ledger, replay diagnostics, and editorial workspace into one release.
