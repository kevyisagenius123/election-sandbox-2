# v0.25D: Editorial analytics workspace

## Summary

v0.25D makes the accepted analytics visible inside the shared Atlas-style laboratory. It connects the Swingometer to the exact scenario-delta ledger and connects the three-state Election Night worker to current-prefix replay diagnostics.

## What changed

- Swingometer now leads with a `What changed?` summary instead of making the user reconstruct the scenario from separate panels.
- Actual, Scenario, and Delta cards state the certified margin, modeled margin, and signed movement.
- A signed operation waterfall separates turnout, preference, and third-party effects.
- County and reporting-unit contributors come from the v0.25B ledger, including residual and off-map partitions.
- Contribution rankings can be filtered by all operations, turnout, preference, or third-party movement.
- A compact methodology drawer explains evidence class, denominator, direction, and geographic treatment.
- The visible PA, MI, and WI replay now adapts its observed event prefix into the v0.25C contract inside the worker.
- Election Night shows five-, fifteen-, and thirty-minute return, ballot, and margin windows, separate progress measures, state openness, chronology stalls, recent movers, and exact window ledgers.
- The Direct Count controls expose a user-selected stall-alert threshold. The threshold changes diagnostics only, never votes or scheduling.
- Desktop and 390-pixel layouts keep the map readable and move dense analysis into responsive editorial surfaces.

## Preserved guarantees

- Swingometer endpoints, replay votes, candidate-blind scheduling, and existing frozen fingerprints remain unchanged.
- Unsupported states do not produce Election Night returns.
- The adapter sends only the observed prefix to descriptive analytics. Hidden future candidate vectors cannot change the current output.
- PA VTDs, MI precincts, WI wards, and off-map units retain their actual evidence and terrain semantics.
- No probability, projection, race call, demographic estimate, backend, membership, or deployment feature was added.
