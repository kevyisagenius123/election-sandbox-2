# v0.23B: Election Night refinement

## Summary

v0.23B makes the integrated three-state Election Night faster to direct and easier to understand without changing its scenario, geography, or evidence boundaries.

## Active-session performance

- The Election Night worker is reused while the replay experience remains open.
- Decoded PA, MI, and WI foundations are retained only inside that active worker.
- At most three scenario endpoint arrays are cached.
- Unchanged state recipes bypass the general behavior allocator and take an exact zero-change path.
- Applying a new chronology restarts from zero without refetching or decoding all three states.
- Returning to the Swingometer terminates the worker and releases the cache.

This is an active-session optimization, not a persistent replay cache. The first entry still loads the detailed state artifacts off-thread.

## Live return explanation

The bottom dock now includes a local return tape. Every visible entry identifies:

- the state and county;
- the number of ballots in the atomic reporting-unit return;
- the replay timestamp;
- the exact net two-party margin movement;
- any statewide lead change caused by that return.

The state cards also display reporting-unit progress bars and a restrained gold pulse on the state that published the newest return.

All explanations use already-published votes. They do not inspect future events, outstanding-vote estimates, projections, or final winners.

## Preserved product law

- Only Pennsylvania VTDs, Michigan precincts, and Wisconsin wards publish visible returns.
- The other 48 jurisdictions remain inert.
- Each legitimate local unit publishes once.
- Timing controls remain candidate-blind and never change votes.
- Swingometer and Election Night continue to share the same mounted deck.gl map.
