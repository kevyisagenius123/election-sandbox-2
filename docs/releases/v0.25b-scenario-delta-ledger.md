# v0.25B: Scenario delta ledger

## Summary

v0.25B creates the exact explanation layer between a Swingometer assumption and its final election consequence.

For any admitted detailed state, the headless ledger answers:

- How many ballots were requested and actually added, transferred, or exchanged?
- How much did each operation move the Harris-minus-Trump margin?
- Which counties and reporting units produced that movement?
- How much movement remains outside mapped terrain?
- Did the state winner or target candidate's Electoral College allocation change?

## What changed

- A canonical state ledger preserves certified, scenario, and signed delta candidate vectors.
- Turnout, preference, and third-party operations each receive an exact candidate and margin attribution.
- Every reporting unit carries certified, scenario, total delta, and three operation delta vectors.
- County rows are rebuilt from those reporting units, with mapped, mixed, and off-map status retained.
- Statewide residual units remain a separate off-map row.
- Mapped, off-map, and statewide-residual partitions disclose their exact contribution.
- Deterministic ranking supports overall, turnout, preference, or third-party movement in absolute, Harris, or Trump direction.
- The ledger embeds a compact v0.25A state analytic collection and references the registered contribution definition for its typed local rows. It does not duplicate a full envelope for every unit.
- Canonical serialization, a frozen golden fingerprint, and semantic validation make the complete ledger reproducible and tamper-evident.

## Preserved guarantees

- Pennsylvania, Michigan, and Wisconsin reconcile independently from detailed units to their accepted scenario endpoints.
- Existing Swingometer arithmetic, contribution UI, replay scheduling, URLs, React, and deck.gl behavior are unchanged.
- No causal demographic claim, probability, projection, race call, backend, or visible workspace was added.
